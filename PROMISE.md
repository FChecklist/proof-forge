# The promise: decision record (2026-10-12)

Owner brief: "complete 12 steps and you will raise funding 100%; funding raised by Step 9."

## Where I disagree, and why
1. **"100% funding" cannot be promised.** The money is a third party's decision. A coaching business that advertises guaranteed funding invites misleading-advertisement and consumer-protection complaints, refunds, and (if a fee depends on money raised) questions under securities and intermediary rules. Lawyer/CA to confirm before any page says it. Also the programme itself tells some founders at Step 1 "needs a new direction": a 100% claim contradicts our own honest verdict.
2. **"Funded by Step 9" is out of order.** Step 9 is the first investor meetings. Between a yes and money sit terms (10), papers (11) and closing (12). Money is real only when it is in the bank (Step 12's own rule).

## What I built instead (js/outcomes.js)
- **Three outcomes**, shown at the end: funding confirmed by the funder, a started company (smoke test + confirmed papers), a way found (gates passed, numbers on proof).
- **Step 9 now needs a live lead**: someone outside offers a follow-up or intro. This is the "funding moving by Step 9" the brief wanted, as a gate that can be checked.
- **Plan B** when there is no lead: shrink the first ask, change route (grant, programme, loan on orders, advance-paying customers), change room, add proof. So the programme always ends in a route, even when the first room says no.
- **Funding ladder**: none, meetings, live lead, signed, money in bank. Only funder-confirmed stages count.
- Intro screen states the honest promise.

## Recommended promise wording (needs owner + lawyer)
"Finish all 12 steps with real proof and you will know exactly where you stand, have a ready company and a sized funding route. If you pass every gate and no outside money is confirmed within [N] months, you get your Rs 19,999 back." The only honest "100%" is the refund, not the funding. N and the refund are owner decisions; at Rs 19,999 with hard gates the refund risk is small.
